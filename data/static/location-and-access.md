The facility is at 15 Railway Square, Yerevan, beside the main station approach. Vehicle entry is from Railway Square; follow the blue parking signs and use the marked entrance lane. The exit lane is separate and returns traffic to Railway Square.

Pedestrian routes lead toward the station concourse and the square. Drivers should keep access lanes, pedestrian crossings, and emergency routes clear at all times.
