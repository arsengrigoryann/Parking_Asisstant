Accessible spaces are positioned near the pedestrian route to the station. Drivers using them should display any locally required accessibility permit and keep the adjacent access aisle clear.

The facility has lighting, marked pedestrian paths, emergency contact points, and routine staff patrols. These measures do not replace normal precautions: lock the vehicle and take valuables with you. Contact on-site staff for accessibility assistance or an immediate safety concern.
