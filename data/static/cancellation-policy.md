Cancellation requests should be submitted before the confirmed arrival time using the same service through which the booking was made. A cancellation is complete only after the service displays a cancellation confirmation.

Any applicable fee or refund depends on the current booking terms shown during confirmation. This public policy does not replace the operational terms attached to a specific booking.
