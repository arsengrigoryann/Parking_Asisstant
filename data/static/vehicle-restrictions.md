The facility is intended for passenger cars, motorcycles, and light electric vehicles that fit safely inside one marked space. Trailers, oversized commercial vehicles, vehicles carrying hazardous materials, and vehicles that obstruct adjacent spaces are not permitted.

Electric-vehicle spaces are reserved for compatible vehicles using the charging area. Motorcycles should use marked motorcycle spaces. Posted entrance clearances and safety signs take precedence when entering the facility.
