Central Station Parking is a staffed public parking facility serving rail passengers, nearby businesses, and visitors to central Yerevan. Clearly numbered spaces include standard, accessible, electric-vehicle, and motorcycle options.

Entry is subject to capacity and the operational status shown by the parking system. Current availability, prices, and opening hours are operational information and should be checked at the time of travel.
