A booking request identifies the intended facility, arrival and departure time, and required space type. A request is not a confirmed reservation until the system explicitly reports confirmation.

Drivers should arrive within the confirmed booking window. Accessible and electric-vehicle spaces may be requested only when their intended features are needed. Current capacity and operational restrictions always come from the parking system rather than this document.
