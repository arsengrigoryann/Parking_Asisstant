## Can I check whether a space is available?

Yes. Availability is live operational data and must be checked through the parking system at the time it is needed.

## Where can I find current prices and opening hours?

Ask the parking service or consult current on-site information. These values can change and are not duplicated in this document.

## Are electric-vehicle spaces available?

The facility includes designated electric-vehicle spaces. Their current status must be checked before arrival.

## Does submitting a request guarantee a space?

No. A space is reserved only after explicit confirmation.

## Who should I contact if equipment is damaged?

Use an on-site assistance point or speak with facility staff. Do not use equipment marked unavailable.
